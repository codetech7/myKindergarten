# Parent Portal Communication Guidelines

## Access Features

### Daily Updates
View real-time updates about your child's activities, meals, naps, and mood throughout the day.

### Photo Gallery
Access photos and videos shared by teachers showcasing classroom activities and special moments.

### Messaging System
Direct communication channel with teachers for questions, concerns, or scheduling requests.

### Calendar Integration
View upcoming events, holidays, parent-teacher conferences, and classroom celebrations.

## Notification Preferences

Customize notification settings for:
- Emergency alerts (always enabled)
- Daily summary reports
- Photo uploads
- Scheduled reminders
- Billing and payment notices

## Security Protocols

All communications are encrypted and access is restricted to authorized guardians only. Photo sharing complies with individual privacy preferences.
