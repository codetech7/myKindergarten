# Student Enrollment Data Schema

## Required Information

### Child Details
- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Gender identification
- Home address
- Emergency contact numbers (minimum 2)

### Guardian Information  
- Primary guardian name and contact
- Secondary guardian name and contact
- Custody arrangements if applicable
- Authorized pickup individuals

### Medical Information
- Allergies and dietary restrictions
- Current medications
- Vaccination records
- Healthcare provider contact
- Insurance details

### Enrollment Preferences
- Desired start date
- Schedule preferences (full-time/part-time)
- Special accommodations needed
- Preferred classroom assignments
