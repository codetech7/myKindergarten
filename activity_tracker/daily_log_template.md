# Daily Activity Log Template

## Arrival Information
- Check-in time
- Guardian who dropped off
- Child's mood upon arrival
- Any special instructions from guardian

## Morning Activities (7:00 AM - 11:30 AM)
- Free play participation
- Circle time engagement
- Structured learning activities
- Outdoor playtime details

## Meal Times
- Breakfast items consumed and quantities
- Snack preferences and consumption
- Lunch menu items and portions eaten
- Fluid intake tracking

## Rest Period (12:00 PM - 2:30 PM)
- Nap start time
- Nap end time
- Sleep quality observations
- Any difficulties settling down

## Afternoon Activities (2:30 PM - 6:00 PM)
- Creative arts participation
- Music and movement activities
- Story time selections
- Closing circle activities

## Departure Information
- Pick-up time
- Guardian who collected child
- Notable highlights to share
- Reminders for next day

## Additional Notes
Space for recording developmental observations, social interactions, behavioral patterns, or concerns requiring follow-up.
