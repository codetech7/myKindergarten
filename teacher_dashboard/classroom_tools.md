# Classroom Management Tools

## Daily Operations

### Attendance System
Record student arrival and departure times with guardian signatures. Track absences and late arrivals.

### Activity Planning
Create and schedule educational activities aligned with developmental goals. Document materials needed and learning outcomes.

### Observation Notes
Capture developmental milestones, behavioral observations, and social interactions throughout the day.

### Communication Center
Send announcements to parent groups, share photos from classroom activities, and respond to guardian inquiries.

## Reporting Features

### Progress Documentation
Generate quarterly developmental assessments covering cognitive, social, emotional, and physical growth areas.

### Incident Management
Document and notify guardians of any accidents, conflicts, or notable events requiring attention.

### Resource Tracking
Monitor classroom supplies, request restocking, and maintain inventory of educational materials.
